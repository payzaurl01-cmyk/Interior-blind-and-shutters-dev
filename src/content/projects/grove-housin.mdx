#### Light, softened

The fabric was selected for the way it catches and filters daylight. A generous full-height drop creates an elegant line while keeping the room bright and relaxed.

#### Privacy when it matters

The layered design gives the household flexibility—open and luminous during the day, then more secluded in the evening.

- Full-height installation for a taller visual proportion
- Soft neutral fabric suited to the interior palette
- Layered light and privacy control
- Smooth, carefully finished tracks

#### A relaxed result

The finished rooms feel lighter and more resolved, with a window treatment that adds warmth without competing with the rest of the interior.
