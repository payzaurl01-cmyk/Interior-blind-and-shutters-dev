#### Effortless control

The blind can be adjusted without reaching across furniture or handling chains, keeping operation simple and the window area visually uncluttered.

#### Designed for rest

The blockout finish helps reduce unwanted light and supports a more comfortable sleeping environment, while the tailored fit improves privacy.

- Quiet motorised operation
- Blockout fabric for stronger light control
- Neat, minimal hardware
- Measured and installed for the individual opening

#### Discreet by design

The technology stays in the background. What remains visible is a refined blind that complements the bedroom and works naturally with everyday life.
